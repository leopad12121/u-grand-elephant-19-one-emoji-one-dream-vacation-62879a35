# Candidates — One Emoji, One Dream Vacation

Gate: 1–8 characters, exactly one emoji, no words. Judge criteria, most
important first: (1) exactly one emoji that plainly names a dream vacation;
(2) evokes the trip vividly at a glance, like a postcard; (3) feels personal
and specific rather than generic; (4) carries a spark of wit or surprise;
(5) reads instantly across cultures and screens.

## The 8 candidates (all composed fresh in this session)

1. "🏝️" desert island — Score 22/25. One glance and you see the place: palm,
   sand, nobody else there. Specific — it is a particular dream, not any
   holiday — and it reads instantly at any size. Strongest of the eight.
2. "🌅" sunrise over the sea — Score 20/25. Beautiful and postcard-like, but
   it could be a morning anywhere; it names a moment more than a trip.
3. "🛫" plane taking off — Score 18/25. Witty — the flight is the dream — but
   it evokes departure, not the destination.
4. "🏖️" beach with umbrella — Score 19/25. Instantly a holiday, but the most
   generic resort image of the eight; any hotel brochure has it.
5. "🐚" shell — Score 16/25. Specific and quiet, but too small a dream: a
   souvenir, not a trip.
6. "🌊" wave — Score 17/25. Evokes the sea well, but it is a surfer's dream
   only; narrower than the island.
7. "🗺️" map — Score 15/25. Adventure, but abstract: a plan, not a place you
   can see yourself in.
8. "🌴" palm tree — Score 18/25. Iconic and instant, but it is one tree; the
   island says the whole place.

## Pick

Candidate 1 — the desert island. It is the most postcard-like of the eight:
a stranger sees the beach, the palm and the emptiness before reading a word,
and it points at one particular dream (getting away to nowhere) rather than
any holiday.
